Writing templates

Use ${name} to insert article information. Other characters stay as written. Unknown names cause an error; missing information becomes empty text.

The same placeholders work in filenames, download subfolders and citation keys. Examples use Harris et al., “Array programming with NumPy”, Nature (2020).

Conversion rules

  • Filenames omit characters disallowed by the OS and end in .pdf. Long names are shortened. Duplicate names receive a number.
  • Download subfolders are inside Chrome’s download folder. Use / for nested folders. Leave blank to save directly in the download folder.
  • Citation keys remove accents and keep only letters, digits and _ : . -.
  • For arXiv, year is the year of the first version.
